# Kanagawa Dragon — theme notes

- Source: https://github.com/rebelot/kanagawa.nvim
- Variant: Dragon (dark, muted) · accent: dragonBlue2
- Generated by `tools/build_theme.py` from `theme.spec.json`.

## Notes

- Variant: Dragon.
- Surfaces follow themes.lua's ui ramp: bg (dragonBlack3) main, bg_dim/bg_m2 (dragonBlack1) panels, bg_m3 (dragonBlack0) rail, bg_p1/bg_p2 (dragonBlack4/5) floating and nested.
- Accent: dragonBlue2 (syn.fun). dragonOrange was considered but sits too close to roninYellow (warning). Links: dragonTeal (syn.special1), ~20° of hue from the accent, underlined.
- Derived: brand_bright = 60% dragonBlue2 + 40% dragonWhite (no lighter blue in the Dragon set). BRAND_560 = waveBlue2 (ui.bg_search).
- Text: one foreground (ui.fg = dragonWhite) for strong and normal; secondary = dragonGray, muted = dragonAsh (syn.comment), muted channels = dragonBlack6 (ui.nontext).
- Status: themes.lua's Dragon diag colours are the Wave ones — samuraiRed, springGreen, roninYellow — so they're used as-is.
- Palette: lua/kanagawa/colors.lua; roles per variant from lua/kanagawa/themes.lua (palette_roles lists every themes.lua role a colour fills in this variant).
